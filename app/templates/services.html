What We Do

Our Services

At CFP Dental, we offer a broad range of services for all ages. We believe that maintaining good dental health is an essential part of a healthy lifestyle.

Preventive Dentistry

Regular check ups and cleaning are essential to maintaining good oral hygiene and dental health. We recommend a dental check up every 6 months in order to detect any early problems such as decay. We will also advise how effectively you are taking care of your teeth and gums between visits.

X-rays are usually taken every 18 months to detect any caries that cannot be seen between your teeth and under existing restorations.

Emergencies

At CFP Dental our priority is to take care of our patients. We will always make an effort to see emergencies as soon as possible and alleviate any pain.

If you have a toothache or dental emergency, please let our team know on booking so that we can make your care a priority.

Restoring Teeth

Teeth can be restored in many different ways. At CFP Dental, we always use tooth coloured fillings. Composite and glass ionomer cement fillings are aesthetic and durable fillings that provide a desirable alternative to traditional amalgam (silver) fillings.

For teeth requiring larger restoration, inlays and onlays can be fabricated. These can be made of composite resin, ceramic or gold.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns are used to restore heavily broken down, root treated and cracked teeth. We use a quality local laboratory to manufacture all our crown and bridgework. This helps ensure the best results for both appearance and durability.

Cosmetic Dentistry

We understand that a beautiful smile can make a world of difference. At CFP Dental, we are able to provide a range of treatment options to improve your smile. This includes teeth whitening, veneers and ceramic crowns.

Anxious Patients

Coming to the dentist can be a nerve wracking experience for some people. At CFP Dental, we make an effort to make your experience as comfortable as possible. Dr Hang has years of experience in helping nervous patients to feel safe and comfortable.

Dental Care for Kids

At CFP Dental, we believe that making children feel confident in their dentist helps set them up for good dental health for life. We are always happy to see kids of any age. Dr Hang is a mum of 3 young boys and enjoys making time to talk with kids about keeping their teeth healthy.
