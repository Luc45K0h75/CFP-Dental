Principal Dentist

Dr Hang Pham

BDSc (UWA)

Hang completed her studies locally at UWA and has over 20 years of experience as a dentist in private practice in Perth. She has also acted as a clinical tutor for final year dental students at the Oral Health Centre of Western Australia.

Although CFP Dental was only established in 2018, Hang has been part of Currambine Family Practice since it started — she is married to CFP principal Dr Tim Koh.

Hang has interests in general dentistry, preventive care and restorative dentistry. As the mother of three boys, she is well equipped to treat children of all ages with patience, calmness and empathy. She also understands the needs of nervous and anxious patients and is adept at putting patients at ease with her approach of kindness and understanding.
